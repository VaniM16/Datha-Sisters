About the Artists

The Legacy of Datha Sisters

Rooted in the timeless Carnatic tradition of South India, Aryadatha K R and Priyadatha K R share a musical intuition born of lifelong sisterhood and rigorous discipleship.

A Dialogue Between Two Violins, A Union of Two Voices

Growing up in Kerala, music was not merely an art form for Aryadatha and Priyadatha—it was their everyday language. Immersed from early childhood in the rich nuances of Carnatic music, the sisters developed a synchronous musical telepathy that makes their live duets exceptional.

Whether bowing the intricate gamakas of Saint Tyagaraja’s kritis or singing evocative verses of Maharaja Swati Tirunal, their performances reflect both deep reverence for Sampradaya (tradition) and the invigorating vitality of contemporary youth.

"When we play together, neither of us leads; we listen to each other until the two instruments dissolve into a single pulse."

— Aryadatha & Priyadatha

Meet The Sisters

Two Artistes, Infinite Expression

@aryadatha
Violinist • Vocalist • Educator

Aryadatha K R

Known for her articulate bowing technique, tonal depth, and commanding stage poise. Aryadatha is celebrated for both lead violin recitals and vocal accompaniment. She is a dedicated educator who offers comprehensive training in both Carnatic violin and vocal disciplines, teaching students both online and offline across the globe.

Violinist Carnatic Vocal Mentor
Follow Aryadatha on Instagram
@priya_datha__
Violinist • Vocalist • Performing Artiste

Priyadatha K R

Priyadatha possesses a naturally sweet, melodic singing voice and exceptional agility on the violin. She is known for her deeply expressive performances and intuitive musical accompaniment, seamlessly partnering with Aryadatha in both energetic, fast-paced exchanges and soulful, slow-tempo melodies. Alongside her performing career, she works as a lecturer in Violin in the Department of Collegiate Education at RLV College, Tripunithura.
